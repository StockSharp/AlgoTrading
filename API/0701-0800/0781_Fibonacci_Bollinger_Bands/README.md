# Fibonacci & Bollinger Bands Strategy
[Русский](README_ru.md) | [中文](README_zh.md) | [Español](README_es.md) | [Deutsch](README_de.md) | [Português](README_pt.md) | [日本語](README_ja.md)

The strategy buys when the price closes below the lower Bollinger Band while remaining above the 61.8% Fibonacci extension from the last swing high and low. The position is closed when the price reaches the upper band or the previous swing high.

**Exact testable rule and defaults:** `BollingerPeriod=20`, `BollingerDeviation=2`, `FibLookback=50`, `FibonacciLevel=0.618`, `CandleType=1 minute`. The band on each completed signal candle is the mean of the last `BollingerPeriod` completed closes **including that candle**, plus/minus the population standard deviation multiplied by `BollingerDeviation`. The preceding swing high and low are the maximum high and minimum low of the **previous** `FibLookback` completed candles, excluding the signal candle. Fibonacci price is `previousLow + FibonacciLevel × (previousHigh − previousLow)` (61.8% measured upward from low; for this simplified implementation it is a retracement-level price, not a projection outside the swing). Enter **long only**, when flat and `Close < lower band && Close > Fibonacci price`. Close the entire long once `Close >= upper band || Close >= previousHigh`; no shorts or stop orders. No indicator may use future bars.
